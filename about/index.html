About

Elena is a research project about a system that learns while it is running, rather than one that is trained and then switched on. This site is the notebook kept alongside that work: what was tried, what held, and what did not.

How to read it#

Every entry carries a status. Open means the question is live. Supported means a prediction held and the observation that would have killed it was given a real chance to fire. Refuted means it fired. Inconclusive means the test could not decide, which is usually a fault in the test rather than in the world.

Refuted entries are kept in full and are listed on the same footing as anything else. A notebook that only records successes is advertising.

The graph#

Entries reference each other, and they reference the papers and books they rest on. Those references are drawn as a map, so it is possible to follow how one result led to the next instead of reading everything in order. Articles are circles. Sources are diamonds. The same information is on that page as a table for anyone who would rather read it, or who is using a screen reader.

Sources#

Every paper and book cited here is listed by reference only: title, author, year and a link to the original at its own publisher. Nothing from them is reproduced on this site. If a source is interesting, it should be read where it lives.

Accessibility#

The site aims at WCAG 2.2 AA and is checked against it on every build. Colour contrast is recomputed from the specification's own formula and a value that fails stops the build. The typeface is Atkinson Hyperlegible, drawn by the Braille Institute for readers with low vision.

Under Reading in the header there are controls for theme, text size, line spacing, line width and typeface, including your own system serif or sans if you prefer one. Those settings are kept in your browser and never leave it. Colour is deliberately not adjustable beyond light and dark, because that is what makes it impossible to configure the site into an unreadable state.

If the graph moves and you would rather it did not, the pause control sits above it, and if your system asks for reduced motion nothing on the page animates at all.

What this site does not do#

No cookies. No analytics. No third-party requests of any kind: the fonts and the one library are served from here. No accounts, no comments, no social links.
